# Supplemental controls

The pause-accent SVG derives from Juxtopposed's pause.svg in the adjacent
coneic-reference directory, recolored for the orange primary play/pause button.
Original design: https://www.figma.com/community/file/1691465999134370980/vlc-redesign
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Original geometry/source hashes are recorded in docs/design-reference-icons.json.

Other SVGs are Coneic's supplemental control geometry, GPL-2.0-or-later, designed
to retain the VLC controls missing from the imported concept export set.
The A/B variants preserve distinct unset, A-set, and A/B-active visual states.
