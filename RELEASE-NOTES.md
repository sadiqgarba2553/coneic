# Coneic Player 0.5.0 for Windows

Download **Coneic-Setup-0.5.0-x64.exe** for standalone installation, or use **coneic-0.5.0-win-x64.zip** with BlueCloud Launcher.

Minimal glass interface, floating playback controls with a live blurred backdrop, personal media library, moments and study tools. General Settings offers system, frosted glass and solid materials. Windows default-player registration and labelled icons cover 134 media formats. The VLC 3.0.24 engine and all 360 plugins are retained.

Existing app identifiers, settings and downloaded content are retained. Windows x64 preview; installers are unsigned. Corresponding source, app metadata and SHA-256 checksums are included.
